Production-feasibility gate

The replacement proof must be small, readable, and buildable.

These eight references are not the Phase 1 production approval target. Before gameplay rendering begins, one replacement set must prove the following constraints together.

  1. 01 One crowded battlefield at actual desktop and phone gameplay scale
  2. 02 Two factions with three representative combat-entity roles each
  3. 03 Unique headquarters plus shared Resource Point and Production Outpost
  4. 04 Ground, navigation, anchor, dynamic, and foreground-occlusion map layers
  5. 05 Four core animation families: idle, move, attack or cast, and defeat
  6. 06 Ownership, selection, and targeting cues that do not rely on color alone

Current decision: no further approval is required for these eight mood references. The next owner review will approve or revise the separate production-scale feasibility proof.
