  1. 01 Battlefield

    A world with routes, obstruction, and territory

    Open full size →

    The map reads as terrain first: authored lanes, flanking space, hard blockers, and landmarks rather than an empty arena. This frame is a two-front visual-language reference, not a validated final layout for two, four, or six players.

    Review: world identity, route clarity, scale, and strategic obstruction.

  2. 02 Opening faction

    Astral Concord

    Open full size →

    A living cosmic-medieval force whose roles are legible through body shape, weapon, posture, and restrained blue-white material language.

    Review: faction identity, humanoid readability, role separation, and tone.

  3. 03 Opening faction

    Gravebound Court

    Open full size →

    An undead counterpart with fractured silhouettes, heavier shadow, violet ritual energy, and an unmistakably different rhythm from the Concord.

    Review: faction contrast, role separation, menace, and visual coherence.

  4. 04 Structures

    Three categories, immediately understood

    Open full size →

    Headquarters anchor each faction; checkpoints communicate territorial control; recruitment structures provide forward production. Ownership changes the same form rather than inventing extra building categories.

    Review: category clarity, ownership states, faction treatment, and scale.

  5. 05 Combat language

    Readable intent before spectacle

    Open full size →

    Selection, focused attack, movement direction, weapon range, impact, defeat, and group spacing remain visible without turning the battlefield into a neon overlay.

    Review: command feedback, formation spacing, attack states, and restraint.

  6. 06 Main menu

    Quiet entry, specific atmosphere

    Open full size →

    The menu is a threshold into this world: one composed landscape, a strong title, and only the actions needed to begin—not a generic card dashboard.

    Review: originality, hierarchy, atmosphere, and minimalism.

  7. 07 Mobile interaction

    Landscape controls that protect the map

    Open full size →

    Touch uses selection, contextual commands, two-finger camera movement, and pinch zoom while the battlefield—not chrome—owns most of the display.

    Review: landscape composition, touch reach, map visibility, and command clarity.

  8. 08 Production and rally

    A producing headquarters owns a compact, visible queue

    Open full size →

    Selecting a producing structure reveals only its roster, current progress, bounded queue, and rally destination. Spawned entities follow an ordinary valid route.

    Review: queue clarity, information density, rally feedback, and map dominance.

Decision gate

Approve the set, or revise by number.

Phase 1 remains unapproved until all eight directions work together. Approval of this draft set records the intended visual direction; asset and animation budgets, provenance, supported aspect range, safe areas, and the device matrix still require their own Phase 1 decisions before gameplay rendering begins.
