01 · Entry and command layer

Keep the quiet menu. Let the map own play.

The owner-retained menu remains the entry target. During play, interface chrome stays at the edges and expands only for the selected entity or producing structure.

HUD contract

  1. 01Persistent edge stripResource, population, and objective state stay compact and readable without covering routes.
  2. 02Selection-owned detailIdentity, health/state, and commands appear only for the current selection.
  3. 03Explicit intentSelection, hostile target, group destination, queue state, and rally use shape plus text—not hue alone.
  4. 04Producer contextA bounded queue exposes progress, order, blocked completion, cancellation feedback, and rally state.
  5. 05No virtual joystickPointer, keyboard, tap, drag, two-finger pan, and focus-centred zoom preserve battlefield space.

02 · World composition

Routes read first. Effects remain bounded.

The approved environment-only plate remains immutable scenery. Structures, combat entities, ownership, navigation, selection, targets, effects, and foreground occlusion are separate runtime concerns even when this static composition shows them together.

Exactly three structure categories

  1. HQFaction headquartersFaction-unique form · starts owned
  2. RPShared Resource PointOne neutral form · capturable
  3. POShared Production OutpostOne neutral form · capturable producer

Six authored layers

  1. 06Foreground occlusion
  2. 05Dynamic entities and effects
  3. 04Structure and objective anchors
  4. 03Navigation and blocker data
  5. 02Non-blocking detail
  6. 01Environment-only ground

Authoritative feedback boundary

  • Simulation ticks own production, contact, damage, capture, defeat, and outcomes.
  • Animation and effects communicate the current result; they never decide it.
  • Every player receives one stable color plus one stable symbol or edge pattern.
  • Selection, target, damage, disabled, capture, and queue states stay distinct from owner color.

Six approved representative base/mask pairs were exported locally with ImageMagick 6.9.12-98 and libwebp 1.3.2 as corrected per-frame lossless WebP sheets. The measured six pairs total 688,988 bytes at 128 and 431,220 bytes at 96; doubling those complete samples projects 1,377,976 and 862,440 bytes for twelve contracts. Both tiers remain below the existing 3.25 MiB hard cap even when shipped together, but only one may be decoded for a match.

Exact browser-decode sample

One representative, both selected tiers.

These four lossless WebP files are bounded review evidence, not shipping atlases. Together they expose the Astral Guardian base and aligned ownership mask at 96 and 128 pixels per cell so the published candidate can verify real browser loading. On 2026-08-21 the lower rows of movement frames 1–3 were restored deterministically from the approved master after the owner identified the incomplete-leg export; the locked upper regions remain unchanged and the repaired masks remain base-contained.

Load failure

No geometric body substitute, rig, wrong-facing art, or missing-mask color fallback. Show a stable text-only local preload error, do not instantiate the entity, and prevent battle start.

Ownership

Base faction materials never recolor. Only restrained mask surfaces receive one of six stable player colors, paired with the same symbol or edge pattern everywhere.

Effects

Effects use separate local assets and budgets. They do not enter the entity atlas, alter contact timing, hide selection/target cues, or become authoritative.
